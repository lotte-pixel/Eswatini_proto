Issue 5 / 2026 · Period: 16 Apr – 15 May 2026 · Issued 15 May 2026
National status: D2 — Severe (emerging) Composite CDI-E: 0.34 Confidence: High (Lowveld) · Moderate (Highveld)

1. National status

Drought conditions are intensifying across the eastern Lowveld and southern Shiselweni. The composite CDI-E reached 0.34 nationally — its lowest May value in the last six years — driven by a 2-month rainfall deficit of −58 mm against the 1991–2020 normal and persistent positive land-surface temperature anomalies (+2.1°C). Approximately 22.6% of the country is now in D2 or worse.

Headline: 22.6% of Eswatini in severe drought (D2+). Lavumisa and Big Bend areas now in D3. Highveld remains stable but vegetation lag is being watched.

4. Where station and satellite signals diverge

Station × pixel comparison is high-confidence in 52% of stations (n=28). Disagreement is concentrated in the north-western Highveld around Piggs Peak and pockets in the Middleveld around Kubuta — consistent with the CHIRPS validation precedent in complex topography. Four stations are flagged Review required and have been routed to the Technical Working Group.

Diagnostic, not error: a disagreement is itself information. The Hub does not force agreement — it raises a confidence flag and explains the likely cause.

5. Sector implications

Agriculture
Maize harvest projection downgraded in Shiselweni & Lowveld; field reports of early tasselling.
Water & WASH
Borehole stress reported in Lubombo; recommend monitoring of communal taps in 4 chiefdoms.
Ecosystems
Vegetation greenness lag in Middleveld; protected-area managers advised.
DRR & preparedness
No Tier-2 escalation yet. Suggest pre-positioning fodder in Shiselweni if D2+ persists 30 more days.

6. Priority watch areas

Immediate monitoring focus: Lavumisa, Big Bend, Nsoko, Siphofaneni, Hluti. Recommend targeted field verification visits for the four "Review required" stations and increased Kobo reporting cadence in the south.

7. Methodology & data sources

CDI-E composite (NDVI + precipitation + LST), station × pixel pairing on 3×3 windows, Pearson/Spearman correlation, MAE/RMSE, drought-class confusion matrix. Sources: NDMC, CHIRPS 2.0, MOD11/MOD13, NDMA 59-station network, KoboToolbox citizen reports, partner data (RSSC, Illovo soil moisture). Full methodology and code: hub.ndma.gov.sz/methods.
